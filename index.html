01 — Website

Tidework

A landing page for a fictional Lisbon synth atelier. The hero is a live oscilloscope "tide" you bend by dragging; the instruments are drawn as to-scale engineering faceplates; the sound section synthesises a patch live. Built in one run, then fixed from its own QA captures.

Ember Run

Skim a dune sea at last light, chain ember ribbons, dodge the spires. Fixed-timestep sim, chase camera with trauma shake and hit-stop, pooled particles, synthesised sound, AgX tone mapping and bloom — the house game kit, playable on keyboard, gamepad or touch.

03 — Motion

Orbital

A 12-second launch opener: a line draws and words rise out of it, an accent wipe carries a counter, a capture grid pops in from the centre and is scanned, the camera zooms through into a kinetic statement, and an orbit draws on to resolve the logo. HTML + GSAP rendered frame-perfect to MP4.

04 — 3D

Rijal Almaa

A stylized procedural model of the stone tower houses of Asir (not a photogrammetry scan): layered stone courses, white quartz bands, painted Al-Qatt shutters and triangular crenellations, rendered in Cycles under a CC0 dusk HDRI and exported as an optimised GLB for the game.

ND96 · Asir

(How)

The studio

01

Start from a kit

Polished, tested starting points for sites, games and films — never a blank file.

02

Decide in writing

A ten-line DESIGN.md: concept, palette, type, motion, the one signature moment, what to avoid.

03

Hero moment first

The best three seconds set the bar for everything else.

04

Look, critique, fix

Real renders on desktop and phone, a blunt art-director critique, then the top fixes — twice.
