(How)

How it's made

01

One-shot concept drafts

A written brief goes to a model and comes back a shipped page. Four briefs produced four TOCK launch sites with nobody editing the words.

02

The designer review loop

A fresh designer reads the real render and scores it out of ten. Nothing ships under 8.5, and the notes come back as defects, not adjectives.

03

Studio-QA gates

Every build is measured, not admired: clipped glyphs, text leaks, line clashes, 390 px overflow, contrast, frame time — desktop and phone.

04

The music library

Thirty-five cleared cues in eight moods, each with its licence and attribution on file and normalised to −16 LUFS.
